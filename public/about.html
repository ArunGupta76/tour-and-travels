Origin Story

From Passionate Backpackers to Global Curators

Founded by passionate explorers in 2011, Wanderlust was built on a simple promise: travel should be enriching, transparent, and seamlessly orchestrated from the moment you book until you return home. Over the past decade, we have proudly served over 50,000 delighted travellers across 25+ global destinations.
